Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

This policy explains how SunnyTV ("the app") handles your information.

Information we access

If you choose to sign in with Google, SunnyTV requests one permission: access to its own app data folder in your Google Drive (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata). The app uses this folder only to store and sync its own settings, such as playlists, favorites and preferences.

SunnyTV cannot see, read, change or delete any of your other Google Drive files.

How information is used

The settings data is used only to make the app work for you across sessions and devices. It is not used for advertising, analytics or profiling.

Sharing

SunnyTV does not sell, rent or share your data with anyone. Your data stays in your own Google account.

Storage and deletion

Your settings are stored in your Google Drive, not on our servers. You can delete them at any time by removing SunnyTV's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can also delete its hidden app data from Google Drive under Settings → Manage apps.

Google API Services User Data Policy

SunnyTV's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
