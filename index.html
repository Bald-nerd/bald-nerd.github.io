SunnyTV

A personal IPTV player for Samsung Smart TVs (Tizen).

SunnyTV lets you play your own IPTV channel lists on a Samsung TV with a simple, remote-friendly interface.

If you sign in with Google, SunnyTV uses your Google Drive app data folder to save and sync its own settings, such as your playlists, favorites and preferences. That folder is private to SunnyTV. The app cannot see, open or change any of your other Drive files.
